Guidance and accuracy

Everything on UniAI is general information to help you plan. This includes university matches and admission categories, scholarship listings, cost and currency estimates, the Visa Guide, deadlines and the guides library. It is not a guarantee, and it is not legal, immigration, financial or admissions advice.

  • Visa and immigration rules change often. The Visa Guide is a summary. Always check the official government sources linked there, or a qualified adviser, before you apply.
  • Match results are estimates from a simple formula. They do not predict whether you will be admitted.
  • University requirements, fees, deadlines and scholarship terms can change. Some data may be a sample or out of date.
  • Exchange rates are indicative. Your bank's rate will differ.
  • When UniAI and an official source disagree, the official source is right.
  • You are responsible for checking eligibility, documents, fees and deadlines before you act.
