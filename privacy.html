The short version

What UniAI does with your information

UniAI is an independent student project, not a company. It collects only what its features need: an account (email, username, role), the searches you run, and, if you use the AI Assistant, the messages you send it. Your shortlist, roadmap progress, settings and chat history are saved in your own browser. We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising or analytics trackers.

Collection

What we collect, and where it lives

Account

Server

Email, username, role (student or counselor) and password. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth, so UniAI never sees or stores your password itself. Your sign-in session is kept in your browser.

University Finder

Server

The country, search text and budget you enter are sent to the UniAI backend to filter universities. Your GPA is used only in your browser to label each result Safe, Target or Dream.

AI Assistant

Server

Your message, the earlier messages in that chat and any saved preferences are sent to the UniAI backend to produce a reply. See the AI Assistant section below.

SOP & LOR tools

Browser

Drafts and evaluations are created in your browser. This version does not send them to a server or save them in your account.

Saved items

Browser

Your shortlist, roadmap checkboxes, theme and settings, and AI chat history are saved in your browser's local storage on this device only.

Cookies and tracking

None

UniAI does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Supabase keeps your sign-in session in local storage.

AI

How the AI Assistant handles your messages

When you send a message, UniAI's backend passes it to an AI model provider to generate a reply, and the reply is shown to you. UniAI does not train any model on your messages. The provider has its own terms for how it handles data it processes, so do not share sensitive details such as passport or ID numbers, bank details, or other people's personal information.

The conversation is also saved in your browser so it is there when you come back. You can delete it with New Chat or with the purge button below.

Other services

Who else may see your connection

Loading the site involves other services. Each one can see your IP address and browser details when your browser contacts it. None of them receive your account details or messages from UniAI.

  • Supabase: account sign-in and session handling.
  • Google Fonts: page fonts.
  • unpkg: delivers the Supabase browser library.
  • Unsplash: destination photos on the home page.
  • exchangerate-api.com: live rates for the Currency Converter (it receives only the currency code).
  • restcountries.com: country list, used only if the UniAI backend is unavailable.
  • placehold.co and university websites: fallback logos and logos loaded from university sources.

Your controls

Delete what is stored on this device

The button below removes UniAI's data from this browser: settings, shortlist, roadmap progress, AI chat history and your sign-in session. You will be signed out on this device. It does not delete your account. To delete your account, see the next section.

Clear UniAI data on this device

Removes local UniAI data and signs you out here. Your account stays until you ask for it to be deleted.

Keeping and deleting

How long data stays

Account details are kept until you ask for the account to be deleted. Browser data stays until you clear it. There is no automatic deletion schedule. To view, correct or delete your account details, email the contact address below from the address you signed up with, and the account will be removed manually.

Younger users

If you are under 18

Many people planning to study abroad are under 18. If you are, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account, and share only what you need to. A parent or guardian can email the contact address below to ask for an account to be deleted.

Changes and contact

Questions or deletion requests

Email contact@YOUR-DOMAIN for any privacy question or request. If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date below will change and the update will be noted on the site.
