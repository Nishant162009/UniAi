01 · Start here

What is a Statement of Purpose?

The idea

An SOP explains why you want this course, what prepared you, and what you will do with the degree. It connects your past, the programme and your future into one honest story.

Why it matters

Marks and test scores show ability. The SOP shows judgement, motivation and fit. For similar profiles, it often decides who stands out.

Names vary: Personal Statement (UK), Letter of Motivation (Germany, France), Statement of Intent. Follow the exact prompt each university gives.
02 · What readers look for

Five things every SOP must answer.

01

Why this field?

Show the moment, project or problem that pulled you in.

02

Why you?

Evidence: coursework, projects, internships, research, work.

03

Why this university?

Name modules, labs, professors or facilities that fit your goals.

04

Why now?

Explain timing, any gaps and how this degree is the next logical step.

05

What next?

Realistic goals after graduation, not vague ambition.

✓

Your voice

Natural, honest English that sounds like a real student.

03 · Structure

A structure that works.

PartPurposeShare of words
Opening hookA specific moment, question or problem5–10%
Academic backgroundRelevant courses, projects, results20–25%
ExperienceInternships, research, work, leadership20–25%
Why this course and universityModules, faculty, fit20–25%
Goals and closingShort and long term plans10–15%
Use this as a starting point. Some universities give their own questions, and then those override this structure.
04 · Weak vs strong

Show, don't claim.

WeakI have always been passionate about computer science since childhood.
StrongerIn Class 11, I built a timetable app for my school after watching teachers lose hours to manual clashes. That project made me want to study algorithms formally.
WeakI am a hardworking and dedicated student.
StrongerI rebuilt our department's lab inventory in a spreadsheet and cut equipment search time from days to minutes.
WeakThis university is world-renowned and has excellent faculty.
StrongerThe Data Mining module and Professor X's work on recommender systems match my final-year project on collaborative filtering.
Examples are illustrations of style. Never copy sample sentences; write about your own real experiences.
05 · Writing plan

From blank page to final draft.

STEP 1

Collect your material

List projects, achievements, challenges, turning points and why each mattered.

STEP 2

Research the course

Note modules, faculty, labs, internships and what makes the programme different.

STEP 3

Outline

Assign material to each part and pick one theme linking them.

STEP 4

Write a rough draft

Do not edit while writing. Aim for honesty, then trim.

STEP 5

Get feedback

Ask a teacher or mentor if it sounds like you and whether claims are specific.

STEP 6

Edit and proofread

Cut repeated ideas, check word limit, read aloud and fix grammar.

07 · Avoid these

Mistakes that sink SOPs.

01

Copying templates

Readers see the same lines again and again.

02

Cliché openings

"Since childhood I have loved..." tells nothing specific.

03

Repeating your CV

Add reasons and reflection, not a list.

04

Generic university praise

Name specific modules or labs.

05

Overclaiming

Do not exaggerate; be ready to explain every line.

06

Ignoring the prompt

Answer the question asked, within the word limit.

08 · Using AI properly

Coach, don't generate.

Good uses

Ask for feedback on clarity, structure and weak spots; brainstorm questions about your experiences; check grammar after you have written your own draft.

Risky uses

Generating the whole statement is a bad idea. It lacks your details, can look generic, and some universities treat AI-written applications as misconduct. Check each institution's policy.

09 · Final check

Before you submit.

✓ Answers every prompt question
✓ Within the word limit
✓ Specific examples, not claims
✓ Tailored to this course and university
✓ Clear goals
✓ No cliché opening
✓ Read aloud once
✓ Proofread by someone else
✓ Saved as requested format
✓ Truthful in every line
