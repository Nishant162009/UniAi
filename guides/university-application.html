01 · Start here

Applying is a campaign, not a form.

What it involves

Choosing courses, preparing documents, writing essays, meeting deadlines and responding to offers. Each step depends on the one before.

Start early

Six to twelve months ahead gives you time for tests, references and funding. Rushed applications show.

Every university has its own rules. Use each official course page as the final reference.
02 · Shortlist

03 · The process

Eight steps to an offer.

STEP 1

Research courses

Compare modules, teaching language, fees, location and entry requirements.

STEP 2

Check requirements

Note grades, tests, deadlines, documents and application fees.

STEP 3

Take tests early

Book language and entrance tests so scores arrive in time.

STEP 4

Prepare documents

Transcripts, CV, essays, references, portfolio if needed.

STEP 5

Apply

Use the portal the university specifies and double-check every field.

STEP 6

Pay and confirm

Keep receipts and confirmation emails.

STEP 7

Interview or tests

Prepare for any interviews, assessments or portfolio reviews.

STEP 8

Decide on offers

Read conditions, compare funding and accept before the deadline.

06 · Offers

Understanding your offer.

Conditional offer

You must meet extra conditions such as final grades or a language score. Track each condition and its deadline.

Unconditional offer

All requirements are met. You still need to follow deposit, funding and visa steps.

Compare offers by course, cost, scholarships and location, not only by name.
