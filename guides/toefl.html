01 · Start here

What is TOEFL, and who needs it?

What it is

The TOEFL iBT is ETS's English test for university admission. It is taken on a computer, either at a test centre or at home with a live proctor (Home Edition).

Who needs it

Applicants whose earlier education was not in English, especially for the USA, Canada and many other destinations. Some universities waive it, so check yours.

The test changed on 21 January 2026. Older prep books and videos may teach the previous format.
02 · Format

Four sections, shorter and adaptive.

R

Reading

Multistage adaptive: question difficulty adjusts to how you perform.

L

Listening

Also adaptive, with shorter audio tasks closer to real university settings.

S

Speaking

Recorded responses in new task types, reviewed after the test.

W

Writing

Redesigned, more structured tasks, with scoring that combines AI and human review.

Sources describe total length as roughly 1.5 to 2 hours. Confirm the exact structure on the official ETS site before you book.

05 · Prep plan

A 6-week plan.

WEEK 1

Diagnose

Use official ETS practice for the new format and note your weakest section.

WEEKS 2–3

Build skills

Read short academic texts daily, listen to lectures and discussions, and learn academic vocabulary.

WEEKS 4–5

Output practice

Record speaking answers and write timed responses, then compare against the scoring criteria.

WEEK 6

Simulate

Take full timed practice tests on a computer with a headset, then rest before the exam.

06 · Booking

Registering and test day.

Registration

Book through the official ETS TOEFL site. One source lists the India fee at INR 16,900, but fees change, so check ETS.

Test day

Bring the ID you registered with, check your headset and audio, and follow the centre or Home Edition rules. Score reporting is faster in the new format; confirm timing with ETS.

07 · Avoid these

Mistakes to skip.

01

Using old materials

Practise the 2026 format only.

02

Ignoring the score scale

Check whether your school wants 1–6 or 0–120.

03

Skipping headset practice

Speaking and Listening depend on audio comfort.

04

No timed practice

Adaptive sections punish slow starts.

05

Booking too late

Allow time for score delivery before deadlines.

06

Assuming every school accepts it

Confirm acceptance and institution codes.
