01 · Start here

Where you live shapes how you study.

Why it matters

Housing affects cost, commute, safety, sleep and friendships. A good choice makes your first term smoother.

Start early

Popular options fill quickly. Start 2 to 3 months ahead where you can, and always arrange temporary stay for the first days.

Never pay money until you have verified the landlord, the property and the contract.

06 · Contract

Read before you sign.

Key clauses

Rent amount and due date, deposit and refund conditions, notice period, repairs, bills and rules on guests or subletting.

Ask questions

If anything is unclear, ask in writing. Keep copies of every message and receipt.

If the contract is in another language, get it translated or checked by a trusted adviser.
07 · Move-in

First-week checklist.

✓ Collect keys and inventory
✓ Photograph every room
✓ Check locks and smoke alarms
✓ Test water, heating and internet
✓ Learn rubbish and recycling rules
✓ Save landlord and emergency contacts
✓ Meet housemates
✓ Register your address if required
