01 · Start here

Where you live shapes how you study.

Why it matters

Housing affects cost, commute, safety, sleep and friendships. A good choice makes your first term smoother.

Start early

Popular options fill quickly. Start 2 to 3 months ahead where you can, and always arrange temporary stay for the first days.

Never pay money until you have verified the landlord, the property and the contract.

04B · Make the call

A simple housing decision flow.

Use this as a shortlist filter—not a substitute for verifying the property and reading the contract.

1. Check university optionsDeadlines, eligibility, support
2. Can you afford the full cost?Rent + bills + travel
3. Verify the propertyLive viewing, identity, ownership
4. Understand the contract?Deposit, dates, exit terms
5. Book with recordsWritten agreement + traceable payment
If any check fails

Pause and verify

Do not send money under pressure. Ask the university accommodation team, a trusted adviser or a local tenant-support service for help.

If all checks pass

Keep a paper trail

Save the listing, contract, inventory, landlord contact details, payment receipts and messages in one folder.

06 · Contract

Read before you sign.

Key clauses

Rent amount and due date, deposit and refund conditions, notice period, repairs, bills and rules on guests or subletting.

Ask questions

If anything is unclear, ask in writing. Keep copies of every message and receipt.

If the contract is in another language, get it translated or checked by a trusted adviser.
