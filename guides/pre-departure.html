01 · Start here

Leaving home is a project, not a trip.

Why a countdown

Visa approval, housing, money, health and packing all depend on each other. Working backwards from your flight date prevents expensive last-minute decisions.

Make it yours

Use this as a base and adjust for your country, university and flight date. Always follow your university's own arrival instructions.

Print or save this page. Tick items off as you go.
02 · Countdown

Your timeline to takeoff.

3 MONTHS BEFORE

Lock the big items

Confirm visa status, accommodation (even temporary), tuition payment plan and funding. Check your passport validity covers your whole stay.

2 MONTHS BEFORE

Money and health

Open or plan a way to pay in local currency. Check vaccination requirements, medical records, prescriptions and insurance.

1 MONTH BEFORE

Book and buy

Book flights, airport pickup and first-week stay. Buy essentials you cannot easily find abroad and start packing lists.

2 WEEKS BEFORE

Paperwork pass

Copy every document, inform your bank, get a phone plan or eSIM ready and check baggage rules.

1 WEEK BEFORE

Final checks

Confirm accommodation arrival time, weigh bags, charge devices and share your itinerary with family.

48 HOURS BEFORE

Airport ready

Online check-in, documents in hand luggage, some local cash, emergency contacts saved offline.

LANDING DAY

Arrive smart

Keep your passport and visa accessible, follow immigration instructions, get to your accommodation and tell family you landed.

04 · Money

Sort money before you land.

Before you fly

Carry a small amount of local currency for the first day, keep a card that works abroad, and tell your bank where you are going. Compare official transfer rates and fees.

First weeks

Open a student bank account, learn how local payment cards work, and set a monthly budget for rent, food, transport, phone and study costs.

Never carry large amounts of cash, and keep one backup payment method separate from your main wallet.

07 · Phone and tech

Stay connected.

Phone

Use an eSIM or local SIM, or keep roaming for the first days. Save university, housing and emergency numbers offline.

Useful apps

Maps and transport, banking, messaging, translation, your university app and a password manager. Enable two-factor authentication.
