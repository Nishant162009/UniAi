01 · Start here

Health cover is not optional in practice.

Why it matters

Healthcare abroad can be expensive and systems work differently from home. Insurance protects your health, your finances and often your visa.

Know your rules

Some countries or universities require specific cover. Check official visa and university pages before buying anything.

This is general guidance, not medical or insurance advice.

05 · Using insurance

How claims usually work.

Before you need it

Save the policy number, insurer contact and claims steps. Know which clinics or hospitals are covered.

When you need care

Keep receipts and reports, follow the claims process and ask what needs pre-approval. Emergencies come first.

In an emergency, get care first and sort the paperwork afterwards.
