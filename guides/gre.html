01 · Start here

What is the GRE?

The test

The GRE General Test measures verbal reasoning, quantitative reasoning and analytical writing for graduate and some business programmes.

Do you need it?

Many programmes are test-optional or no longer require it. Check each department's current policy.

Never assume it is required or waived. Confirm on the programme page.
02 · Format

Three sections.

SectionTestsScale
Verbal ReasoningReading comprehension, vocabulary in context130–170
Quantitative ReasoningArithmetic, algebra, geometry, data analysis130–170
Analytical WritingArgument and issue essays0–6
The test has section-level adaptivity: your performance in the first part shapes the difficulty of the second.
03 · Scores

What scores mean.

Targets

Programmes publish typical ranges. Aim for the middle or higher of their admitted profiles.

Validity

Scores are generally valid for five years. Check each institution's rules.
