🏛️ Why Study in Germany?

Germany is home to some of the world's most prestigious technical and research universities. Most public universities charge zero tuition fees for both EU and non-EU international students, requiring only a nominal administrative semester fee (typically €150–€350).

Whether you study engineering at TU Munich, humanities at Humboldt University Berlin, or business in Frankfurt, you gain access to state-of-the-art research facilities and strong industry connections with global brands like BMW, Siemens, and SAP.

💶 Living Costs & Blocked Account

While public tuition is free, international students must prove financial self-sufficiency. As of 2024/2025, you must deposit €11,208 into an official German Blocked Account (Sperrkonto), which pays out roughly €934 per month for living expenses.
