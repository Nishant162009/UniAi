01 · Start here

Is France the right study destination for you?

France can offer public universities, specialist schools, global cities, research opportunities, cultural life and programmes in both French and English. The best choice depends on your subject, language level, budget, career goals and preferred student environment.

Why students choose France

Students can consider public universities, engineering schools, business schools, art and design institutions, and specialised programmes across many cities.

France is not one single higher-education experience. Your institution type, city and language of instruction can change your whole journey.

Think beyond rankings

Compare programme modules, teaching language, internship links, assessment, tuition, accommodation, city costs and graduate opportunities.

A strong course fit is usually more useful than choosing only by a famous name.

03 · Understand the system

French education, explained simply.

Before applying, understand the qualification type, expected duration, teaching language and the institution’s own requirements.

L

Licence

Usually an undergraduate qualification completed after secondary education, commonly structured across three academic years.

M

Master

Postgraduate study usually following a Licence, often designed to deepen academic or professional knowledge.

D

Doctorat

Research-focused study completed under academic supervision, centred on original research and a doctoral project.

PATH

Preparation routes

Language preparation, pathway programmes or other routes may help students meet entry conditions where available.

08 · Admissions

What do French institutions look at?

Requirements change by institution, programme, qualification level and applicant profile. Use each official course page as your final reference.

01
Academic qualifications

Institutions assess your marks, transcripts and previous qualification against their entry standards.

02
Language ability

French and/or English proficiency may be required depending on the programme language.

Some programmes request a CV, motivation letter or evidence of relevant experience.

04
References

Academic or professional references may be requested, especially for postgraduate applications.

13 · Accommodation

Where will you actually live?

CROUS housing

Student residences may offer useful options but can be limited and competitive, so apply as early as possible.

Private residences

Purpose-built student housing can provide furnished rooms and managed services at different price points.

Private rental

Shared flats and private rentals offer flexibility, but check deposits, guarantees, bills, contracts and commute time.

Before transferring money, confirm the property is genuine, understand the contract, check what bills are included and use secure payment methods.
14 · Life in France

Studying is only half the experience.

Language

French can help with daily life, internships, administration and local friendships—even if your degree is taught in English.

Transport

Metro, tram, bus and rail networks vary by city. Research the commute before signing a housing contract.

Healthcare

Understand the student health-registration and insurance arrangements relevant to your own immigration situation.

Food and budgeting

Cooking, student restaurants, markets and a monthly budget can make day-to-day spending easier to manage.

Student communities

Join societies, sports clubs, cultural groups and international-student activities to build a support network.

Academic habits

Plan for independent study, coursework, presentations, group projects and assessment styles that vary by programme.

17 · Avoid these mistakes

The problems usually start before the flight.

Mistake 01

Choosing by prestige without checking the exact course content, teaching language and entry requirements.

Mistake 02

Calculating tuition but forgetting housing deposits, transport, setup costs and emergency savings.

Mistake 03

Waiting too long to apply for scholarships, housing or language preparation.

Mistake 04

Assuming every French institution uses the same application process or fee structure.

Mistake 05

Leaving visa tasks until after travel plans are already fixed.

Mistake 06

Ignoring the value of basic French for housing, daily errands, internships and social life.
