06 / Visa runway

From acceptance letter to konnichiwa.

The exact process depends on your program and circumstances. Use this as a planning flow and confirm current requirements through official Japanese sources and your institution.

STEP 01

Choose your program

Compare degree programs, language of instruction, intake, location, admission requirements and costs.

STEP 02

Apply + receive admission

Prepare academic documents, language evidence where required and the materials requested by your institution.

STEP 03

Certificate of Eligibility

For many student visa cases, the institution supports the process for obtaining the Certificate of Eligibility.

STEP 04

Student visa application

Follow the current instructions from the Japanese embassy or consulate responsible for your application.

STEP 05

Land in Japan

Sort housing, registration, transport, banking and orientation — then start your new routine.
